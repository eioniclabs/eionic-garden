What this page shows, and what it does not.

Positions per tick, the action, the zone and the errands are engine output. With timeline.jsonl, the walk duration comes from the engine (distance divided by gait speed) and the anchors are the engine’s. Two things are presentation, not simulation: when inside the tick a walk starts (a hash draw per avatar and tick; no RNG is consumed) and the straight line of the walk (the engine moves one straight vector per tick; the real path belongs to the client’s navmesh). Without a timeline file this page falls back to a straight-line glide over the whole 4 hours, which is a display artifact. When two avatars talk, their talk windows may not overlap in time; a client should start a conversation only when both are present. The page replays saved logs and does not re-run the engine.
